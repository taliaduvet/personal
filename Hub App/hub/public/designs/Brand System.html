— 01 / voice

how it sounds.

we write the way talia talks: warm, plain, a little dry, sometimes vulnerable. small letters by default. no marketing words ("seamless," "unlock," "revolutionize"). no exclamation marks unless the sentence earns one.

we say —
  • "built between songs."
  • "for the 3am idea you forget by morning."
  • "one-time purchase. rent-to-own if money's tight."
  • "healing isn't a straight line. neither is making things."
we don't —
  • unlock your creative potential
  • the all-in-one platform for artists
  • supercharge your workflow
  • enterprise-grade reliability

— 03 / color

two surfaces, one accent.

cosmic for the public hub. paper for working surfaces (admin, workspace doc). violet is the only "loud" color — used sparingly so it stays meaningful. the four palette tones are pulled from talia's promo photos and used across the site as environmental flavor: stars, accents, halos, fog. they're never tied to a specific product or release.

do —
use the palette as flavor — mix the four tones across stars, halos, section markers, fog. let the page feel like it's made of all of you, not just one color.
don't —
use the tones for CTAs, body text, or large fills. those stay violet (house) or neutral. don't pin one tone to one product either — they're free-floating.
why —
the four tones came from photos of you. mixing them everywhere keeps the site feeling like it belongs to a single person, not a brand committee.
— 04 / typography

three families, one mood.

Instrument Serif carries the emotional weight (headlines, prices, product names). Inter does all the working text. JetBrains Mono signals "tech / nav / label." Caveat is reserved for hand-annotations in admin/workspace — not for the public hub.

— display · instrument serif (regular + italic)
neither is making things.
fallbacks: cormorant garamond, georgia, serif · use italic for emotional emphasis · letter-spacing -0.015em
— body · inter
built between songs, for solo artists, neurodivergent creatives, and everyone else still trying to make something. one‑time purchase. rent‑to‑own if money's tight.
