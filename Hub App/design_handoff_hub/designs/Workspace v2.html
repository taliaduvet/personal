— § 01 / spec & understanding

what vein is.

a voice-memo vault for solo artists. capture audio fragments fast, transcribe with whisper, mark moments inside recordings, and link them to songs as you work. files live in the user's google drive — we never hold them.

★ claude's understanding re-summarized 14m ago · from this page
"here's what i think vein is — correct me where i'm off."
  • 01vein is a capture-first tool — the record button is the home screen. everything else is downstream of getting an idea down before it slips.edit
  • 02users are solo musicians and songwriters, often neurodivergent, working between other things. it needs to feel calm and forgiving more than precise.edit
  • 03fragments (marked moments) are the unit of value. a memo is just a container.edit
  • 04recordings live in the user's own Google Drive. we keep a local copy so it works without internet. ownership before convenience.edit
  • 05transcription uses Whisper, only when the user asks. retries automatically if it fails. transcription has a real cost — we'll probably set a monthly limit later.edit
  • 06songs are collections of fragments. you link them from inside a memo. one fragment can belong to more than one song.edit
  • 07pricing: $300 once, or $5+/month rent-to-own up to 24 months. turns on at v1.5. free during beta.edit

— non-goals (what vein is NOT)

not a daw. not a song-sharing platform. not a streaming service. not a "music ai." not a collaboration tool (yet — see roadmap v1.6).
